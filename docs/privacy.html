Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

In short. PaceTasks stores the tasks you type so it can show them to you. It has no ads, no trackers and no analytics, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing. An account is optional. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What PaceTasks does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of your data, to anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location or calendar.
  • Dictation into Brain Dump uses your phone keyboard's own microphone button; PaceTasks never records audio and only receives the resulting text.

Brain Dump and AI

Today, Brain Dump text is read by PaceTasks' own rule-based parser on our server. It is not sent to any AI company. If a future version uses an AI provider to understand your text better, this policy will be updated before that version is released and will name the provider.

Notifications

The evening reminder is scheduled on your phone by the app itself. No notification content passes through our server.

Where your data is kept and who helps process it

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in. The PaceTasks database is located in the European Union (Ireland).
  • An email delivery service sends the one-time sign-in codes to the address you entered.
  • Apple distributes the app and, if you use Sign in with Apple, confirms your Apple ID to us; its own privacy policy covers the App Store and Sign in with Apple.
  • GitHub hosts this website (not the app's data) and, like any web host, logs visits to it.

These services process data only to provide the app. Data is encrypted in transit, and each user can read and change only their own rows in the database.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Storing your tasks, settings and account: needed to provide the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Tips and product news by email: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw in Settings at any time.
  • The fair-use limit on Brain Dump: our legitimate interest in keeping the service available (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

You can see and change your tasks, settings and email consent in the app. To erase everything, open Settings → Your data → Delete account (or Delete my data if you have no account): your account, tasks, statistics, settings and Brain Dump text are removed from our database immediately. Any backups kept by our hosting provider expire on their own schedule.

You also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected, to restrict or object to its use, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. Write to the contact address above for any of these. Without an account we can only identify your data from inside the app on your phone, so the in-app deletion is the way to erase it.

Children

PaceTasks is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
