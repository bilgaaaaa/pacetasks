Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

In short. This policy covers two apps sold together: PaceTasks for iPhone and DeskFocus for Mac and iPhone. PaceTasks stores the tasks you type so it can show them to you. DeskFocus keeps everything on your devices and talks only to Apple and, if you connect it, Google. Neither app has ads, trackers or analytics, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing. An account is optional. You can delete everything from inside the apps at any time.

Who is responsible

PaceTasks and DeskFocus are made by Bilge Ozcanbaz, an individual developer based in Padova, Italy, who is the data controller. Contact: pacetasks@bilgalog.dev.

What PaceTasks does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of your data, to anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location or calendar.
  • Dictation into Brain Dump uses your phone keyboard's own microphone button; PaceTasks never records audio and only receives the resulting text.

Brain Dump and AI

Today, Brain Dump text is read by PaceTasks' own rule-based parser on our server. It is not sent to any AI company. If a future version uses an AI provider to understand your text better, this policy will be updated before that version is released and will name the provider.

Update check

When it opens, the app asks Apple's public App Store service which version of PaceTasks is available, so it can tell you about an update. The request contains only the app's identifier and your phone's region, nothing about you or your tasks.

Notifications

Reminders (the daily check-in and the reminders you set on tasks) are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. No notification content passes through our server.

Where your data is kept and who helps process it

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in. The PaceTasks database is located in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Resend sends the one-time sign-in codes and, if you switched them on, the tips emails to the address you entered.
  • Apple distributes the app and, if you use Sign in with Apple, confirms your Apple ID to us; its own privacy policy covers the App Store and Sign in with Apple.
  • GitHub hosts this website (not the app's data) and, like any web host, logs visits to it.

These services process data only to provide the app. Data is encrypted in transit, and each user can read and change only their own rows in the database.

DeskFocus

DeskFocus has no account and no server of its own. What it reads stays on your Mac or iPhone, or goes directly between your device and Apple or Google.

To let you pick the apps to hide during focus, DeskFocus lists the apps in your Mac's Applications folders. Only the apps you pick are saved, and only on your Mac.

What DeskFocus does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No account with us, and nothing sent to our server.
  • While you focus, it hides or quits the apps you picked; it never reads what is inside them.
  • The "time is up" notification is scheduled on your device by the app itself.

Google user data

DeskFocus' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It is used only to show and update your own calendars and tasks inside DeskFocus, is never transferred to us or anyone else, and is never used for advertising. You can remove DeskFocus' access at any time in DeskFocus Settings or in your Google account.

Where DeskFocus comes from

GitHub hosts the DeskFocus download for Mac and, like any download host, logs the request. Apple distributes the iPhone app; its own privacy policy covers the App Store and TestFlight.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Storing your tasks, settings and account in PaceTasks, and reading your calendars and tasks in DeskFocus: needed to provide the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Tips and product news by email: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw in Settings at any time.
  • The fair-use limit on Brain Dump: our legitimate interest in keeping the service available (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

You can see and change your tasks, settings and email consent in PaceTasks. To erase everything, open Settings → Your data → Delete account (or Delete my data if you have no account): your account, tasks, statistics, settings and Brain Dump text are removed from our database immediately. Any backups kept by our hosting provider expire on their own schedule.

DeskFocus keeps nothing on our side. Disconnecting Google in its Settings and deleting the app removes what it stored on your device.

You also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected, to restrict or object to its use, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. Write to the contact address above for any of these. Without an account we can only identify your data from inside the app on your phone, so the in-app deletion is the way to erase it.

Children

PaceTasks and DeskFocus are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
