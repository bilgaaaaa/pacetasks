Help and support

Answers to the questions that come up most.

Need a hand? Write to ozcanbazbilge@gmail.com. A person reads every message.

Do I need an account?

No. PaceTasks works the moment you install it. An account (Settings → Create account) keeps your tasks safe if you change or lose your phone, and lets you open them on another one.

I didn't get my sign-in code

The code can take a minute to arrive; check your spam folder too. In the app you can ask for a new code or go back and correct the email address. Each code works once and expires after a short while.

How do I add many tasks at once?

Tap the microphone next to Add a task… and type or dictate everything on your mind, in English, Italian, Turkish or a mix. PaceTasks shows what it understood before anything is added.

I don't know where to start

On Today, tap Pick my next task. Decide for me shows you a single task, and Fit my time lists the tasks that fit the time and energy you have.

What happens to tasks I didn't finish?

The next day, a row on Today says how many tasks were carried over. Tap Review to see where PaceTasks would move each one: today, tomorrow, the weekend or Someday. Change any of them, then apply.

My reminder doesn't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for PaceTasks in your phone's Settings, and that Evening review is switched on in the app's Settings.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Open Settings → Your data → Delete account (or Delete my data if you have no account). Everything is erased from our database straight away, and it cannot be undone.

Can I try PaceTasks before installing it?

Yes. The web demo runs the whole app in your browser with sample tasks. Nothing you type in it is saved, and it starts fresh each time you reload.

What does PaceTasks do with my data?

It stores your tasks to show them to you, and nothing more: no ads, no trackers. The details are in the privacy policy.
