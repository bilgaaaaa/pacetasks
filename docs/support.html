Help and support

Answers to the questions that come up most.

Need a hand? Write to pacetasks@bilgalog.dev. A person reads every message.

Do I need an account?

No. PaceTasks works the moment you install it. An account keeps your tasks safe if you change or lose your phone, and lets you open them on another one. Open Settings → Create account and enter your name and email, or tap Continue with Apple. There is no password, and the tasks you already have stay.

I didn't get my sign-in code

The code can take a minute to arrive; check your spam folder too. In the app you can ask for a new code or go back and correct the email address. Each code works once and expires after a short while.

How do I add a task?

Tap Add a task… at the bottom of Today and type the name. The pills under it set the day, how long it takes and the time; the small options pill holds part of the day, category and energy. Before you type, your usuals sit underneath: tasks you have done before, one tap to add again. A name you have used before fills in its usual details.

How do I set a start time and a reminder?

In the add sheet, tap the time pill and choose Set a start time. Move the hour and minutes with the arrows, or pick one of the quick times. Under it, Remind me sets the nudge: Off, at the start, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes before, or 1 hour before. The line below tells you exactly when it will arrive. A task with a start time also gets a Focus pill on Today.

Can I plan a task for a later day?

Yes. Tap the day pill and pick one of the next seven days, or open Whole month for any later date. You can also choose no exact day: Any day keeps it on Today until it is done, and This week or Next week sets a deadline on that Sunday. A task set for one exact later day waits in Upcoming, at the foot of Today, and comes to Today on its day.

How do I add many tasks at once?

Tap the microphone next to Add a task… and type or dictate everything on your mind, in English, Italian, Turkish or a mix. PaceTasks shows what it understood before anything is added.

I don't know where to start

On Today, tap Pick my next task. Decide for me shows a single task: a quick win while there is one, then whatever matters most. Fit my time asks how much time and energy you have and lists the tasks that fit, back to back.

What happens to tasks I didn't finish?

The next day, a slim row on Today says how many tasks were carried over. Tap Review and each one has a suggested new home: Today, Tomorrow, the Weekend or Someday. Change any of them, then apply. When tasks keep slipping, Weekly Reset at the foot of Today walks you through them one at a time.

My reminder doesn't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for PaceTasks in your iPhone's Settings, and that the switches are on under Settings → Reminders: Daily check-in for the daily nudge, and Task reminders for the ones you set on tasks. The daily check-in is sent only on days when tasks are still open. Reminders are planned on your phone a few days ahead, so open PaceTasks now and then to keep them coming.

Can I change the colours?

Yes. Settings → Look sets light, dark or the same as your phone, and the colour theme: Sage, Pastel Night or Sunny. Sage also lets you pick an accent colour.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Open Settings → Your data → Delete account (or Delete my data if you have no account). Everything is erased from our database straight away, and it cannot be undone.

What does PaceTasks do with my data?

It stores your tasks to show them to you, and nothing more: no ads, no trackers. The details are in the privacy policy.
