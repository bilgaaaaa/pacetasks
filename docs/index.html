    A day with PaceTasks

    Work fills the middle of the day. PaceTasks makes the most of the edges.

    1. 07:40Tuesday, before work

      Ten minutes before you leave

      Tap Pick my next task. Decide for me shows one task: a quick win while there is one, then whatever matters most. Fit my time lists everything that fits the minutes and energy you have.

      Tap a task's minutes to start a timer. It turns green once you are in your usual range.

    2. 12:30Lunch break

      Empty your head in one go

      Tap the microphone and type or dictate everything on your mind, in English, Italian, Turkish or a mix. PaceTasks splits it into tasks, understands “tomorrow morning” or “by Friday”, and shows what it understood before anything is added.

      water the plants tomorrow morning, domani comprare il pane, cuma günü faturayı öde

    3. 17:45The nudge you picked

      New

      A start time, and a nudge before it

      Adding a task is one card now: the name, then a pill each for the day, how long it takes and the time. Set 18:00 and choose when to be reminded: at the start, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes before, or an hour ahead.

      The time also places the task before or after work and gives it a Focus session. Need a later date? Open the whole month, or just pick This week.

    4. 07:30Wednesday, the next morning

      Didn't finish? No guilt pile

      One slim row says how many tasks were carried over. Open it and each one has a suggested new home: Today, Tomorrow, the Weekend or Someday. Change any of them, then apply.

      Ideas you are not ready for go to Someday, so they stop crowding your day.

    5. 19:00Sunday evening

      Reset the week, then see your pace

      Weekly Reset walks you through what keeps slipping, one task at a time. Your pace shows your streak, your best day and 13 weeks at a glance.

      After ten finished tasks, My Pace tells you in plain sentences when you really get things done, and whether tasks take longer than you guess.

    Your tasks stay yours

    • No sign-up. Open the app and start. Your tasks are kept under an anonymous ID.
    • No ads, no trackers, no analytics. Your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
    • Kept in the EU. The database is in Ireland, and only you can read your rows.
    • An account only if you want one. First name, last name, email and a code, or Continue with Apple. No password.
    • Reminders stay on your phone. They are scheduled by the app itself, never sent by our server.
    • Delete everything from the app. Settings, then Your data: erased from our database straight away.

    The details are in the privacy policy.

    And the small things

    • Your usuals. Tasks you have done before, one tap to add again.
    • Quick wins. Anything five minutes or less is highlighted, and you set the limit.
    • Focus sessions. A Pomodoro countdown that logs the real minutes.
    • Daily check-in. One quiet nudge, only on days with tasks still open.
