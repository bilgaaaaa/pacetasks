Tell me what I need to do.

PaceTasks figures out how to fit it into your life. Catch personal tasks the moment you think of them, then get them done before or after work, with the time and energy you really have.

Try it in your browser

Can't decide? It picks for you.

Tap Pick my next task. Decide for me shows one task: a quick win while there is one, then whatever matters most. Done logs how long it took and offers the next.

Fit my time asks how much time and energy you have, from 5 minutes to an hour, and lists the tasks that fit back to back.

Say everything at once.

Tap the microphone and type or dictate whatever is on your mind, in English, Italian, Turkish or a mix. PaceTasks shows the tasks it understood before anything is added.

“Tomorrow call the vet, domani comprare il pane, cuma günü faturayı öde”

Unfinished tasks find a new day.

Yesterday's leftovers don't pile up. One row on Today proposes where each should go: today, tomorrow, the weekend or someday. Change any of them, then apply.

When tasks have waited too long, Weekly Reset walks you through them one at a time.

Compete only with your own pace.

Streaks, minutes, a 13-week heatmap and how close your estimates come to reality. After ten finished tasks, My Pace tells you in plain sentences when you get the most done.

Your tasks stay yours.

  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics. Your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • No account needed. PaceTasks works the moment you open it. Add your email later to keep your tasks when you change phones, with a sign-in code instead of a password.
  • Delete everything from the app. One option in Settings erases your tasks and account from our server straight away.
  • Brain Dump stays with PaceTasks. Your text is read by PaceTasks' own rules, not sent to an AI company.

Questions

When can I get it?

PaceTasks is getting ready for its first beta on iPhone. Until then, the demo runs the whole app in your browser with sample tasks.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. The first version is for iPhone only.

Do I need an account?

No. An account is optional and keeps your tasks safe if you change or lose your phone.

Does the demo keep what I type?

No. It runs entirely in your browser on sample tasks, and everything resets when you reload.
