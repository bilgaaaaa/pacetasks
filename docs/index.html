    One list, from your pocket to your desk.

    PaceTasks works out when each task fits into your day. DeskFocus keeps you on it once you sit down. Here is a day with both.

    1. 7:45On your iPhone

      Say everything at once.

      On the way to work, tap the microphone and type or dictate whatever is on your mind, in English, Italian, Turkish or a mix. PaceTasks shows the tasks it understood before anything is added.

      “Tomorrow call the vet, domani comprare il pane, cuma günü faturayı öde”

    2. 9:00On your Mac

      Today is already on your desk.

      DeskFocus puts your iCloud Calendar and Reminders, Google Calendar and Google Tasks on your Mac desktop as one list, and your PaceTasks list is coming soon. It shows as a widget, in the menu bar or as a glass panel, so what's next is always a glance away.

      Today's list comes from

      • iCloud Calendar
      • iCloud Reminders
      • Google Calendar and Tasks
      • PaceTasks Coming soon

      One list on your desktop

    3. 10:30On your Mac

      One task, one timer, nothing pulling you away.

      Press Start on any task and its timer runs on your desktop. The apps you choose are hidden until the session ends, or quit in strict mode. When the time is up, Done ticks the task off where it came from.

      Hide while you focus

      The widget at the top of the page follows these switches.

    4. 12:40On your iPhone

      Can't decide? It picks for you.

      Tap Pick my next task. Decide for me shows one task: a quick win while there is one, then whatever matters most. Fit my time asks how long you have, from 5 minutes to an hour, and lists the tasks that fit.

    5. Tomorrow, 8:00On your iPhone

      Unfinished tasks find a new day.

      Yesterday's leftovers don't pile up. One row on Today proposes where each should go: today, tomorrow, the weekend or someday. Change any of them, then apply. When tasks have waited too long, Weekly Reset walks you through them one at a time.

    6. SundayOn your iPhone

      Compete only with your own pace.

      Streaks, minutes, a 13-week heatmap and how close your estimates come to reality. After ten finished tasks, My Pace tells you in plain sentences when you get the most done.

    Questions

    When can I get them?

    DeskFocus is ready to download for Mac. PaceTasks is getting ready for its first beta on iPhone; until then, the demo runs the whole app in your browser with sample tasks.

    Do I need a Mac to use PaceTasks?

    No. PaceTasks works on its own on your iPhone. DeskFocus adds your Mac desktop for the hours you sit down to work.

    When will my PaceTasks tasks show up in DeskFocus?

    Soon, in an update to both apps. Today DeskFocus shows iCloud Calendar, iCloud Reminders, Google Calendar and Google Tasks.

    Which Macs does DeskFocus run on?

    Any Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

    Is DeskFocus on the Mac App Store?

    No. DeskFocus is a direct download: open the file and drag DeskFocus into Applications. After that it keeps itself up to date.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes, within 14 days of being charged, for any reason. The refund policy says how for App Store and Paddle purchases.

    Is there an Android version?

    Not yet. PaceTasks is for iPhone, and DeskFocus for Mac.

    Does the demo keep what I type?

    No. It runs entirely in your browser on sample tasks, and everything resets when you reload.
