    It Picks for You.

    Can't decide? Tap Pick my next task.

    Decide for me shows one task: a quick win while there is one, then whatever matters most. Fit my time asks how long you have, from five minutes to an hour, and lists the tasks that fit.

    Included in PaceTasks

    Unfinished Tasks Find a New Day.

    Yesterday's leftovers don't pile up.

    One row on Today proposes where each unfinished task should go: today, tomorrow, the weekend or someday. Change any of them, then apply. When tasks have waited too long, Weekly Reset walks you through them one at a time.

    Today's numbers are free. With Pro, My Pace adds your week and your history: streaks, minutes, a 13-week heatmap and how close your estimates come to reality, and after ten finished tasks, plain sentences about when you get the most done.

    Questions.

    When can I get them?

    DeskFocus is ready to download for Mac. PaceTasks is getting ready for its first beta on iPhone; until then, the demo runs the whole app in your browser with sample tasks.

    Do I need a Mac to use PaceTasks?

    No. PaceTasks works on its own on your iPhone. DeskFocus adds your Mac desktop for the hours you sit down to work.

    When will my PaceTasks tasks show up in DeskFocus?

    Soon, in an update to both apps. Today DeskFocus shows iCloud Calendar, iCloud Reminders, Google Calendar and Google Tasks.

    What does Pro cost?

    €24.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, €3.99 a month, or €59.99 once for good. The App Store shows the price for your country. One Pro covers PaceTasks and DeskFocus; planning your day stays free.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes, within 14 days of being charged, for any reason. The refund policy says how for App Store and Paddle purchases.

    Is there an Android version?

    Not yet. PaceTasks is for iPhone, and DeskFocus for Mac.

    Does the demo keep what I type?

    No. It runs entirely in your browser on sample tasks, and everything resets when you reload.
