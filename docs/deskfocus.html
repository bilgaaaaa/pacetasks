    Three Steps to a Focused Day.

    1. 01

      Connect your lists

      Allow Calendar and Reminders, and sign in to Google once. Today's events, reminders and tasks appear in the widget.

    2. 02

      Pick what matters now

      Choose one task from today's list, or from any day, and press ▶ to start its timer.

    3. 03

      Focus, then check it off

      Your chosen apps step aside while the timer runs. When the time is up, press Done and the task is ticked off.

    Questions.

    Where do my tasks come from?

    From iCloud Calendar, iCloud Reminders, Google Calendar and Google Tasks; each calendar or list can be switched on or off. Your PaceTasks list is coming soon.

    What happens to other apps during focus?

    The apps you choose are hidden while a timer runs, or quit in strict mode, and come back when the session ends. An optional macOS Focus mode silences notifications too.

    Does it come with PaceTasks?

    Yes. DeskFocus for Mac and PaceTasks for iPhone are sold together: plan on your phone, focus at your desk. The DeskFocus widget is free, and one Pro covers both apps; see the pricing.

    Is it on the Mac App Store?

    No. DeskFocus is a direct download from this site: open the file and drag DeskFocus into Applications.

    How does it stay up to date?

    It looks for a new version once a day and updates itself in place, keeping your settings and tasks. You can check right away, or turn the daily check off, in Settings → General → Updates.

    Which Macs does it run on?

    Any Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later. On macOS 26 the desktop panel uses Liquid Glass.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes, within 14 days of being charged, for any reason. The refund policy explains how.
